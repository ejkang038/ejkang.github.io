Pearls

2026

Case Study

Solo Project

Strategy · UX Research

UI Design · Brand

Your doctor gave you a pill, No one gave you a guide.

Pearls is a PCOS wellness app that connects daily pill tracking, cycle insights, and supplement recommendations built for women who deserve more than a prescription.

← →

UX Strategy

Core Decision

01Positioning: From Fertility to Energy & Focus+

Reframed around everyday energy and focus, not fertility, for young women managing wellness in busy lives.

02Business Model: Free Tracking as the Engine of Commerce+

Track to Trust to Buy: free tracking powers personalized commerce (no subscriptions, no ads), a harder path competitors avoid, still under validation.

03AI as a Dedicated Tab, Not a Floating Agent+

A session scoped tab with no saved history, trading conversational continuity for trust in a sensitive health context.

04Medical Validation: A Deliberate Next Step+

Clinical review and a drug to symptom database are intentionally left as the next step toward a real launch.

05Branding: Tech Sensibility on a Warm Base+

A warm base with 3D product shots and glassmorphism accents, expressing "medical but not clinical" through color and texture.
